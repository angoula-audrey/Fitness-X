Our Team

Alex Rivera – Strength & Conditioning Coach

Alex specializes in functional movement, resistance training, and athletic performance. He designs personalized workout routines tailored to individual fitness levels, corrects movement techniques to prevent injuries, and helps clients build lean muscle mass and physical endurance safely and efficiently.

Marcus Vance – High-Performance Executive Coach

Marcus works with professionals to optimize their physical energy, daily productivity, and stress resilience. He bridges the gap between career demands and health, teaching busy leaders how to maintain top cognitive performance and stamina under high-pressure environments.

Sarah Chen – Mindset & Life Coach

Sarah empowers individuals to overcome mental barriers, manage stress, and build sustainable healthy habits. Through targeted coaching sessions, she helps clients clarify their personal goals, increase daily discipline, and cultivate a positive growth mindset essential for long-term personal transformation.

Dr. Elena Rostova – Registered Clinical Nutritionist

Dr. Elena evaluates individual metabolic needs, dietary restrictions, and health goals to create science-backed, customized meal plans. She educates clients on proper macronutrient balance, gut health, and sustainable eating habits that fuel workouts, enhance recovery, and promote lifelong wellness.
